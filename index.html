How to play

Making connections

Find relationships between the words. Choose a connector, then drag from a source word to its destination, or select the two words in order. Arrowheads show the direction of each connection.

Select an existing connection’s label to change its connector. Repeat a linked pair to remove it. Press Escape to cancel a drag. When you’re ready, select Check my web to compare your connections and explore the pathway.

Exploring the board

The web adapts and fits automatically. Zoom in, then drag the background to explore. Select Fit web to see the whole web. Hover or focus a word or connection label for a larger preview.
